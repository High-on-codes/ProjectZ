Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Plain terms first, precise terms second.

1

Who we are

Project Z is published by Celsitudo Studio, a sole proprietorship of Noaman, based in India ("we", "us"). We are the data controller (called "Data Fiduciary" under India's DPDP Act) for the personal data described below. Contact: see Contact & grievances.

This policy covers the Project Z iOS app and this website.

In short: the goals and courses you create stay on your device. We run no server that stores them. The only personal data we may hold is an email address, if you choose to give us one for updates or support.

Information we do not collect

  • The goals, prompts or courses you create in the app
  • Your usage patterns, session length or feature interactions
  • Advertising identifiers or data for ad targeting
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos or other device content

The app contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK or third-party crash reporter. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

4

How on-device processing works

When you type a goal, Project Z generates the course using Apple's on-device intelligence frameworks. Course generation does not use the network. The result is saved in a local SwiftData store in the app's sandbox on your device.

Apple may include that app data in your device backups (iCloud or local), under Apple's own terms and encryption. Deleting the app or a course removes it from your device. Existing backups are managed by Apple and may retain the data until they are overwritten or deleted in your Apple account settings.

Because we never receive your content, we cannot view it and cannot recover it if it is lost.

5

  • Formspree, Inc. (United States) processes email submissions from our form on our behalf.
  • Apple, Inc. processes App Store purchases, subscriptions and opt-in crash diagnostics under its own privacy terms.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

We use no advertising network, analytics platform or data broker.

6

Legal bases for processing

  • Consent, for emails you submit through our form. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests (answering your support request; keeping the website secure).
  • Contract, for purchase confirmations needed to provide premium features.

Where India's DPDP Act applies, we process data on the basis of your consent or other lawful uses it permits.

7

Retention & deletion

We keep form-submitted emails until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and delete them when the purpose is complete. Support emails are kept for up to 12 months after the issue is resolved. Server logs are kept for a short period set by our host.

Course and goal data is not held by us, so there is nothing on our side to delete. See section 4 for deletion on your device.

8

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal data
  • Object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time
  • Complain to your data protection authority (for example in the EU/EEA, UK, India, Brazil, Canada, Australia or Japan)
  • EU/UK (GDPR): the rights above, plus the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • India (DPDP Act): the right to access information, correct and erase data, grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise rights on your behalf
  • Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), Australia (Privacy Act), Japan (APPI): equivalent access, correction and deletion rights under local law

To exercise a right, contact us. We respond within one month (or the period required by your local law). We may need to verify your identity.

9

International transfers

We are based in India. Emails you submit through our form are processed by Formspree in the United States, so your data is transferred outside your country. Where required (for example from the EU/EEA or UK), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy mechanism that the processor provides. Your goal and course content never leaves your device, so it is not transferred.

10

Children

Project Z is intended for users aged 15 and over (or the higher minimum age in your country). If you are under the age of digital consent or majority where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission to use the app or to give us an email address. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

11

Security

Form submissions are sent over TLS. On-device data is protected by iOS sandboxing and file-system encryption. Since we hold no central database of course content, none can be breached from our side. No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires.

12

AI notice

Courses are generated by an AI model running on your device. Output can be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable. It is not professional advice. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect is made about you by automated means.

13

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last revised" date, add an entry to the change log, and notify you in the app or by email where the law requires.
